Overview

QuickScan & Craft scans, creates, and customizes QR codes and barcodes on Android. The app processes the contents of scans and generated codes on your device. We do not operate a developer server or require an app account, and we do not sell the contents of your codes. This policy describes the ads-free release, including the data handled by Google ML Kit and Google Play Billing.

1. On-Device QR & Barcode Processing and ML Kit Telemetry

The app uses bundled Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning (17.3.0) to recognize codes and ZXing (3.5.4) to generate them. The app does not upload your camera frames or the contents of your codes, such as links, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, event information, or text, for processing.

As described in Google’s ML Kit data disclosure, the SDK sends diagnostics and usage analytics to Google over HTTPS. These can include:

  • Device and hardware details, such as model, Android version, and available ML accelerators.
  • The app’s package name and version.
  • Operational information such as processing times, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature versions, recognition events, and error codes.
  • An identifier for the app installation, used for diagnostics and usage analytics. This is not Android’s Advertising ID and is not intended to identify a person or physical device uniquely.

Google states that this ML Kit data is not shared with third parties. QuickScan & Craft uses manual camera zoom and does not enable ML Kit’s auto-zoom feature, so the SDK does not collect the additional telemetry associated with that feature.

2. Permissions We Request & Why

  • Camera: Runs the live scanner. The app analyzes camera frames in memory and does not save or upload the live camera feed.
  • Location (optional): Used when you tap Current Location to fill in coordinates for a Location QR code. The app does not access background location. Coordinates used for the code stay on your device unless you choose to share them, for example by sharing the code.
  • Vibration: Provides scan feedback if enabled in Settings.
  • Internet and network state: Support ML Kit diagnostic reporting and Google Play Billing.
  • Calls and messages: The app does not request permission to make calls or send SMS. When you choose to act on a scanned number, message, email address, or link, Android hands it off to an appropriate app.

3. Local Storage, Retention & Android Backups

Scan and creation history and saved style presets are stored in the app’s private database. This database, onboarding state, and the local Premium purchase cache are excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Settings such as theme and scan feedback, and locally stored custom logo images, may be backed up by Android if backup is enabled. Temporary export files are stored in cache directories excluded from backups.

Your retention and deletion controls

In Settings → History & Privacy, turn off Save Scan & Create History to stop saving new history, or use Clear History to remove existing scan and creation history. You can also delete individual history entries and saved presets. Clear History does not remove your presets, settings, temporary export files, or copies already saved or shared outside the app.

Uninstalling removes the app’s private local data. It does not erase eligible Android backups, files saved outside the app, or copies already shared with other apps. Neither uninstalling nor clearing history deletes telemetry or transaction records already processed by Google.

4. Advertising

This release does not request or display ads. It does not include the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, and it does not request the Android Advertising ID permission.

5. Google Play In-App Purchases

Google Play Billing (9.1.0) processes the one-time Premium upgrade. QuickScan & Craft does not receive your card number, bank account information, or billing address. The app checks purchase ownership with Google Play to unlock or restore Premium. It receives purchase tokens and order identifiers through Play Billing and caches Premium ownership status and the last order ID in private local storage to support offline access. That cache is excluded from Android backup.

6. Exports & External Apps

When you share an export, Android grants the receiving app temporary read access to the selected file. When you save an export, the app writes a copy to the location you choose in Android’s file picker. Clearing history or uninstalling does not remove copies saved or shared outside the app.

7. Data Security

ML Kit diagnostic traffic and Google Play Billing communications are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Android’s per-app storage sandbox isolates the app’s private local files.

8. Children’s Privacy

QuickScan & Craft is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
