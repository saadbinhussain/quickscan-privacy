Privacy Policy for QuickScan & Craft

Effective / Last Updated: November 2026
Application: QuickScan & Craft (com.oronyx.quickscancraft)
Developer Contact: saadbinhu@gmail.com

Overview

QuickScan & Craft is a QR code and barcode scanner, generator, and customization app for Android. Your QR scans and created codes are processed directly on your device—we do not operate developer servers, do not require user accounts, and never collect, store on our servers, or sell your scan results or generated payloads.

The app bundles official Google SDKs that handle on-device barcode recognition (with standard Google SDK diagnostics), consent management, optional Free-tier banner advertisements (when configured), and Google Play Billing over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections as described below.

1. On-Device QR & Barcode Processing and ML Kit Telemetry

  • Local Barcode & QR Processing: Optical barcode detection (via bundled Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning 17.3.0) and QR/barcode matrix rendering (via ZXing 3.5.4) run locally in your device's memory. Scanned links, Wi-Fi passwords, contact vCards, calendar events, and custom text are never uploaded to developer servers, Google ML Kit servers, or advertising networks.
  • ML Kit SDK Diagnostics & Usage Data: Consistent with Google's official ML Kit Android data disclosure, the bundled ML Kit Barcode Scanning SDK automatically transmits technical diagnostics and usage analytics to Google over encrypted HTTPS/TLS to maintain performance, debug errors, and detect abuse (and does not transfer this diagnostic data to third parties). This SDK telemetry includes:
    • Device & Hardware Information: Device manufacturer, model, Android OS version/build, and available ML hardware accelerators.
    • Application Information: App package name and version.
    • Performance & Operational Metrics: Processing times, API configuration metadata, input/output sizes, feature versions, initialization/recognition events, and error codes.
    • Per-Installation Identifier: ML Kit uses a per-installation identifier for diagnostics and usage analytics. It is not intended to uniquely identify a user or physical device and is separate from Android’s Advertising ID.
  • Auto-Zoom Disabled: QuickScan & Craft does not enable ML Kit's enableAutoZoom() option (camera zoom is manual only via the on-screen slider), so ML Kit auto-zoom session telemetry (dynamic auto-zoom session IDs, zoom changes, and predicted bounding box coordinates) is not collected.

2. Permissions We Request & Why

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely to operate the live barcode scanner viewfinder. Camera frames are analyzed ephemerally in volatile memory and immediately discarded; no photos or video streams are saved or uploaded.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — Optional): Requested only when you explicitly tap "Current Location" while creating a Location QR code so the app can prefill latitude and longitude. Coordinates remain on your device unless you choose to export or share the resulting QR code. Background location is never accessed.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used solely for optional haptic feedback when a code is scanned, if enabled in Settings.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used by Google Play Billing, Google UMP consent checks, Free-tier banner advertising (when enabled), and Google ML Kit SDK diagnostic reporting.
  • Zero Call or SMS Permissions: Tapping a scanned phone number, SMS, email, or link opens your device's default app chooser (ACTION_DIAL, ACTION_SENDTO, ACTION_VIEW) without granting this app call or SMS permissions.

3. Local Storage, Retention & Android Backup Behavior

  • Scan & Creation History + Custom Style Presets (quickscan_database): Stored privately inside Android's sandboxed Room (SQLite) database on your device (history_items table for scan/create history and presets table for custom style presets). This database is excluded from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer (backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml).
  • Install & Billing Cache Files: Onboarding state (quickscan_install_state.xml and noBackupFilesDir/onboarding_completed.flag) and cached Premium purchase preferences (quickscan_billing_prefs.xml) are excluded from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer so fresh installs verify entitlement directly with Google Play.
  • Settings & Custom Embedded Logos: Non-sensitive preferences (theme, sound, vibrate, history recording toggle in user_settings.preferences_pb / quickscan_user_settings.xml) and custom logo images you embed into QR codes (files/logos/) are stored locally in app internal storage and may be backed up by Android OS Backup if enabled in your Android device settings.
  • Temporary Export Caches: Ephemeral files generated during Share or Batch Export (PNG, SVG, PDF, ZIP) live in temporary cache directories excluded from backups.

Your Retention & Deletion Controls

  • Toggle Save Scan & Create History off at any time in Settings > History & Privacy.
  • Delete individual history entries or custom style presets inside the app.
  • Clear all saved scan and creation history items (history_items) via Settings > History & Privacy > Clear History. Clear History removes saved history items only—it does not delete your saved custom style presets (presets), app settings, temporary export caches, or files you previously saved to device storage or shared with other apps.
  • Uninstalling QuickScan & Craft removes the app's local sandboxed data from your device, but does not erase eligible Android OS backups (if enabled on your device for app preferences or custom logos), files you previously saved via Android's Storage Access Framework, or copies already delivered to other apps via Android Sharesheet.
  • Local Deletion vs. Off-Device SDK Data: Clearing local history or uninstalling the app affects your device only and does not delete off-device telemetry or transaction records previously processed by Google ML Kit, Google AdMob, or Google Play.

4. Advertising & Consent (Free Tier)

QuickScan & Craft includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Next-Gen SDK (1.6.0) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP 4.0.0) to display non-intrusive banner advertisements on main navigation tabs for Free-tier users when production AdMob IDs are configured.

  • Data Processed by AdMob (when ads are active): Consistent with Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, Google AdMob may automatically collect and share with Google and participating ad partners:
    • Approximate Location (derived from IP address)
    • App Interactions (such as app launches, ad impressions, and ad taps)
    • Diagnostics (performance, crash, and energy-usage telemetry)
    • Device or Other Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) (which users can reset or delete in Android System Settings) and App Set ID (used for analytics and fraud prevention; not reset or opted out via Android Advertising ID controls).
  • Consent & Ad Privacy Choices: Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable jurisdictions), UMP presents a consent form before ads load. Ad Privacy Choices is available in Settings when required by UMP consent rules and applicable to the build's ad configuration. Tapping History & Privacy > Privacy Choices opens the UMP privacy options dialog when production ads are configured and consent management applies. You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in Android System Settings.
  • Premium Users & Unconfigured Builds: While Premium is active, the app does not request or display banner ads. When production AdMob IDs are not configured in a build, the advertising and consent SDKs are not initialized.
  • Privacy Guarantee on QR Content: Your scanned barcodes and created QR payloads are never sent to AdMob or any advertiser.

5. Google Play In-App Purchases

One-time upgrades to QuickScan & Craft Premium are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (9.1.0). We never receive, access, or store your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing address. The app queries active INAPP entitlements (queryPurchasesAsync), receives purchase ownership tokens and order identifiers (orderId) over encrypted TLS to unlock Premium and restore past purchases via Google Play, and caches ownership state and the last orderId locally in quickscan_billing_prefs.xml (excluded from backup) for offline continuity.

6. Exporting, FileProvider & External Apps

When you share an export, Android gives the receiving app temporary read access to the selected file. When you save an export, QuickScan & Craft writes a copy to the location you choose in Android’s file picker. Copies saved or shared outside the app are not removed when you clear History or uninstall the app.

7. Data Security

  • All communications with Google ML Kit diagnostics, Google Play Billing, Google UMP, and Google AdMob are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Local data is isolated within Android's per-app storage sandbox.

8. Children's Privacy

QuickScan & Craft is a general-purpose utility app not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
